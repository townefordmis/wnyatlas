### Cross-site limits

The records do not establish a common waste source, shared plume, a direct Love Canal connection, or attribution of Cayuga Creek's dioxin/PFAS findings to either site. Keep those questions and contaminant-specific evidence separate. A cap, cover, delisting recommendation or registry code does not by itself establish unrestricted cleanup or that a water pathway has ceased to function.
