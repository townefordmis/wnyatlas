# Verified campus / cleanup-property relationships

Publication scope revised 2026-07-29.

The schools map excludes ordinary 500-foot and 1,000-foot proximity. A campus is
included only when an authoritative record establishes that a cleanup property is
part of the campus or directly adjacent.

## Included campuses

### South Buffalo Charter School — 154 S Ogden Street

- Relationship: campus developed on the documented 154 South Ogden Street
  Brownfield Cleanup Program property.
- DEC site: C915268.
- Primary support: DEC Decision Document and Brownfield Cleanup Agreement.

### Buffalo School of Culinary Arts / PS 42 — 75 W Huron Street

- Relationship: the school occupies the building historically identified as
  73-75 W Huron within the 73-79 W Huron Brownfield Cleanup Program property.
- DEC site: C915282.
- Primary support: DEC Brownfield Cleanup application, Decision Document, and
  completion records.

### Waterfront Elementary School — 95 Fourth Street

- Relationship: DEC records state that part of the former Buffalo Service Center
  property was acquired for the school; the connected Former Buffalo Service
  Station and Fourth Street records identify cleanup areas immediately adjacent
  to the campus.
- DEC records retained as one connected story: V00362, 915167, C915194, and
  C915194A.
- Primary support: DEC Interim Remedial Measure Work Plan for C915194 and Fourth
  Street Site Record of Decision.

## Excluded from this map

All other Buffalo-address campuses and nearby screening records. Their absence
does not make a statement about the presence or absence of historical land use or
environmental conditions. Relevant cleanup locations remain available through the
main regional WNY Atlas.
