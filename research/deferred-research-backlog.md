# WNY Atlas deferred research backlog

This file preserves promising research topics that are intentionally not ready for publication.
An item being listed here does not mean that WNY Atlas has verified the claim or decided to
build a map for it.

## Health studies and reported disease patterns

**Status:** Deferred for a future deep dive. No health map or new health claims should be
published yet.

### Questions to review

- Cancer incidence investigations in all eight WNY Atlas counties
- Thyroid cancer and thyroid-disease reports
- Parkinson's disease prevalence and reported geographic patterns
- Multiple sclerosis prevalence and reported clusters
- Historical occupational disease investigations
- Whether any official study evaluated a relationship between a health pattern and a specific
  environmental site, exposure pathway, or time period

### Starting records already identified

- NYSDOH East Buffalo/Western Cheektowaga cancer-incidence investigation, using 2011-2015
  incidence data
- NYSDOH Love Canal Follow-up Health Study, including cancer incidence from 1979-1996
- NYSDOH county and regional Parkinson's disease and parkinsonism dashboard
- National Medicare geographic studies of Parkinson's disease
- Historical occupational multiple-sclerosis research involving a zinc-using manufacturing
  plant near Rochester, outside the current eight-county map

### Required research before publication

- Review official NYSDOH, county health-department, CDC, ATSDR, and cancer-registry records
- Review peer-reviewed epidemiological studies and subsequent follow-up work
- Record the study population, geographic boundary, observation years, and exposure years
- Record observed and expected cases, rates, confidence intervals, and statistical significance
  when available
- Separate diagnosed cases, insurance-claims prevalence, modeled estimates, community
  reports, and formally investigated clusters
- Account for age, sex, population movement, healthcare access, diagnostic practices, latency,
  occupational history, and other limitations discussed by the researchers
- Determine whether each finding is current, historical, unresolved, or contradicted by later
  research
- Obtain a stable primary source for every statement considered for publication

### Publication safeguards

- Do not map individual patients, homes, or inferred case locations
- Do not call an area a "cluster" unless the responsible health agency or a well-designed study
  supports that description
- Do not infer exposure or causation from proximity to a cleanup site
- Do not draw an impact boundary unless the source defines one
- Clearly distinguish elevated incidence, a statistically significant finding, an association,
  and a demonstrated cause
- Include the official conclusion and limitations, even when they complicate the story
- Keep health research on a separate map or research experience if enough qualified records
  eventually justify one
- Link to the main environmental atlas only when a source documents the relationship

### Current preliminary decision

The evidence presently supports detailed research notes for Love Canal and the East
Buffalo/Western Cheektowaga investigation. It does not yet support publishing a general WNY
health-cluster map. Parkinson's information is currently better treated as county-level
surveillance with limitations, while no adequately documented local thyroid or multiple
sclerosis cluster has yet been identified within the eight-county atlas area.

## How to add future topics

For each deferred topic, record:

1. The question being investigated
2. Why it may fit the WNY Atlas mission
3. The geographic and historical scope
4. The best primary sources already found
5. What remains unverified
6. The evidence required before publication
7. The eventual decision: publish, hold, or exclude
