# Catholic Academy / Prince of Peace: historical review and open questions

Reviewed November 16, 2026. The comparison with Donovan Head Start concerns evidence stages, not a shared waste source. No validated contemporary campus soil radionuclide or radon results were verified in this review.

## Original records verified

1. [ORNL/RASA-85/1, Results of the Mobile Gamma Scanning Activities in Niagara Falls, New York Area](https://lmpublicsearch.lm.doe.gov/LMSites/2708-NY.17-9_NFSS.pdf). Title page: manuscript completed February 1985; issued August 1985. Introduction: mobile survey conducted October 3–16, 1984. Table 4, printed page 12 / PDF page 17, names Prince of Peace Church, Military Road, with rectory at 908 North Military Road; identifies Ra-226 anomaly type and a parking-lot property. Table visually inspected. Do not describe 1985 as the field-survey date.
2. [ORNL/TM-10076, Results of Radiological Measurements Taken in the Niagara Falls, New York, Area](https://lmpublicsearch.lm.doe.gov/LMSites/2726-NY.17-10_NFSS.pdf), published November 1986. Table 5, printed page 23 / PDF page 30, identifies the church parking lot as Anomaly #59: 43 µR/h at one meter, 77 µR/h at the surface, scan range 29–77 µR/h. Table 11, printed page 30 / PDF page 37, marks #59 with footnote f, **not sampled**. There is no property-specific soil concentration in that table. Table 14, printed page 38 / PDF page 45, describes a church parking lot, Ra-226 anomaly type, not NFSS-related, asphalt context. These three table pages were visually inspected; row identity, units and footnote were checked together.
3. The 1986 report's significance discussion, printed pages 5–6, describes regional phosphate slag used beneath pavement and a reported Oldbury Furnace origin. It is not a shipment record or laboratory identification of present material at this campus. Do not infer the school's soil composition from the regional interpretation, the Ra-226 anomaly classification or Donovan's confirmed slag.
4. [BTPM, November 11, 2026 on-site reporting and EPA coordinator interview](https://www.btpm.org/local/2026-11-11/epa-begins-follow-up-radiological-testing-on-grounds-of-niagara-falls-church-and-school). Supports September exterior screening, slightly elevated readings in parking areas beneath pavement, November 11 soil sampling and interior follow-up. The coordinator's screening-based no-immediate-threat statement is dated and qualified; it is not a completed laboratory or exposure assessment. The report anticipated a wait for analytical results; no later validated campus laboratory dataset was located in this review.
5. [EPA Donovan assessment](https://www.epa.gov/ny/donovan-head-start-removal-site) documents the 2021 discovery and December assessment, uranium-238-bearing slag at approximately 6–24 inches beneath playground/parking areas over approximately 10,000 square feet, relocation and interior/radon findings. The page still describes some cleanup steps prospectively; use the [EPA completed-removals summary](https://www.epa.gov/ny/niagara-county-radiation-removal-sites) for the completed 2023 removal and late-summer restoration. Do not mistake an older project-plan page for the latest cleanup status.

## Publication decisions

- Expand the existing Catholic Academy record; retain research-in-progress status and its current school-address map point.
- Add the original 1985/1986 sources and dated measurement context. Label the historical dose-rate quantities as gamma exposure rates, not soil concentrations, current readings or cumulative doses to children.
- Improve the existing historical Anomaly #59 marker's name, measurement fields and caution. Keep its existing approximate address-based coordinates; no new boundary or claimed exact overlay with the current campus is added.
- Link Catholic Academy and Donovan reciprocally through a comparison connection. Keep Donovan's completed removal distinct from Catholic Academy's unresolved assessment.

## Open research queue

1. **Historical-to-modern footprint:** Obtain original survey sketches/field logs, rectory address history, deeds and parcel maps to match 908 North Military Road to current campus buildings and 2026 sample locations. Do not infer an exact hotspot match or contaminated area from the church name, school address or nearby map points.
2. **Children's outdoor spaces:** Obtain the complete survey coverage map, scan logs, sampling rationale, depths, pavement/soil coverage and inaccessible locations. Determine whether playgrounds, lawns and all outdoor activity areas were assessed. Missing public coverage documentation proves neither omission nor contamination.
3. **Current analytical findings:** Obtain validated soil radionuclide analyses, mineralogy, QA/QC, detection limits, interior scoping and radon results, and dated agency interpretations. Distinguish scheduled tests, actual sampling, preliminary screening and released results. Do not transfer historical #59 readings to 2026 conditions.
4. **Material and source:** Test the phosphate-slag hypothesis against physical material descriptions, radionuclide profiles, fill contracts, hauling records and agency conclusions. No common source with Donovan, Oldbury, Bethlehem Steel or TAM is established by the reviewed evidence.
5. **Regulatory history:** Recover prior property investigations, notification correspondence, decisions and any remediation or redevelopment records. A historical anomaly alone does not prove continuous conditions, uninterrupted exposure or agency inaction since the 1980s. Not NFSS-related is not a clean-property certification or a completed removal determination.
6. **Donovan closeout comparison:** Retain the existing queue for final disposal quantities, destination and verification/closeout reports. Do not characterize its removal as completion of every regional radiological investigation.
