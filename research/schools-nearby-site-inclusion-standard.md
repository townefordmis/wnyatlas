# Schools map: nearby-site inclusion standard

Working editorial standard. The proximity screen is a research queue, not the
published map.

## Required tests

A nearby record is included only when all four tests are met:

1. **Authoritative identity**
   - The location and site code are confirmed by an official DEC, EPA, municipal,
     or equivalent primary record.
2. **Mission relevance**
   - A primary source documents at least one meaningful part of the WNY Atlas
     mission: former industry; waste disposal; a transformed waterway; a documented
     release; investigation or remediation; site management; or brownfield
     redevelopment.
3. **Geographic relevance**
   - The relationship to a mapped school campus is verified as same property,
     shared boundary, across a right-of-way, within 500 feet, or within 1,000 feet.
     The public record states the relationship as distance/context only.
4. **Explainable history**
   - The available sources support a short factual account of what was there, why
     the site entered an environmental program, what action occurred, and the
   current documented program status. Unknown portions are labeled.

Successful remediation is itself mission-relevant history. A completed cleanup,
Certificate of Completion, closed site with continuing management, or documented
remedial action is not removed merely because the work succeeded. These records
should explain what occupied the property, what was found, what was done, when the
work occurred, and whether monitoring or controls remain.

## Editorial outcomes

- **Include — full history:** All four tests pass and the source record supports a
  complete concise history.
- **Include — connected record:** The record is an off-site operable unit or a
  second program record that is necessary to explain a retained parent site. It is
  presented with the parent, not as a duplicate location.
- **Hold for research:** The map relationship is real, but accessible sources do
  not yet support the required historical account.
- **Exclude from schools map:** The relationship is a screening artifact,
  duplicate, outside the chosen distance, or lacks a mission-relevant documented
  history.

## Language boundary

- Proximity is not exposure.
- A cleanup-program listing is not a finding about a school.
- Ownership is not responsibility.
- A listed contaminant is not evidence that it reached a school.
- Completion, site management, and continuing controls are reported together;
  “cleaned up” is not used as a substitute for the agency's documented status.

## Duplicate handling

The following should normally be one historical story with multiple linked agency
records:

- on-site and off-site portions of the same cleanup;
- a State Superfund record and Brownfield Cleanup record for the same property;
- multiple Brownfield Cleanup parcels within one documented industrial complex;
- multiple program records for one waterway project.

The underlying site codes remain visible in the source section.
